# Optional product 3D models

Place optimized `.glb` product models here when available, for example `models/products/custom-gaming-pc.glb`.

The catalog has an optional 3D viewer. In Admin, enter the relative `.glb` path in the product's **Optional 3D model path** field. The catalog shows **View 3D** for products with a model, and loads the viewer library only when a customer clicks it. Keep a regular WebP photo as the product-card image and fallback.

A model needs to come from CAD/Blender or be reconstructed from a multi-angle photo set; one ordinary product photo cannot show accurate hidden sides. Use optimized GLB models and test them before publishing, since large models take longer to download on mobile connections.
